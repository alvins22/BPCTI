Admissions

Admissions & Tuition

BPCTI offers specialized professional communication training designed around practical learning outcomes and career-relevant communication competencies.

Prospective students are encouraged to review available specializations, discuss their professional goals, and complete any applicable placement assessment before enrollment.

Admissions Process

Five Steps to Enrollment

  1. Step 1

    Explore the Programs

    Review the English and Korean programs and their professional specializations.

  2. Step 2

    Select Your Specialization

    Identify the course most relevant to your professional objectives.

  3. Step 3

    Request Information

    Contact BPCTI regarding scheduling, course availability, and admissions requirements.

  4. Step 4

    Complete Placement Guidance

    Participate in a communication assessment or consultation when required.

  5. Step 5

    Review Enrollment Documents

    Review the applicable catalog, tuition, payment terms, cancellation and refund policies, and course requirements before making an enrollment commitment.

Placement & Proficiency Guidance

Choose Your Specialization. Find Your Appropriate Training Level.

BPCTI recognizes that professional communication needs differ according to language proficiency, work experience, and career objectives.

Applicants select the specialization that best matches their professional goals. A placement and training-needs assessment helps BPCTI recommend an appropriate instructional level or class.

Depending on the specialization, assessment may consider:

  • Current communication proficiency
  • Formal versus conversational language skills
  • Professional and leadership experience
  • Relevant workplace communication needs
  • Career development objectives
  • Readiness for applied professional training
Selecting a specialization does not require completing another specialization. Placement recommendations and any applicable proficiency prerequisites are communicated before enrollment.
Financial Support

Scholarships & Employer Education Benefits

Limited need-based scholarships and institutional tuition assistance may be available to eligible students, subject to eligibility requirements, available funding, program availability, and BPCTI policies. Scholarship assistance is not guaranteed.

Some employers offer education assistance or tuition reimbursement. Eligibility and approval are determined by each employer's own process; BPCTI does not guarantee that any course qualifies for employer or third-party education benefits.
