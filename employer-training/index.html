Professional Communication Training for Teams and Organizations

BPCTI welcomes conversations with organizations that want to strengthen how their employees communicate in interviews, meetings, client relationships, and cross-cultural collaboration.

Contact BPCTI

Training Grounded in Real Workplace Situations

BPCTI's English and Korean specializations are organized around the professional situations employees face every day. Organizations can contact BPCTI to discuss their team's communication needs, which specializations may fit, and whether group or scheduled training can be arranged.

Each conversation starts with your goals. BPCTI can then share information about course availability, placement guidance, and scheduling.

Areas of professional focus

  • Interview, promotion, and career advancement communication
  • Business meetings, virtual collaboration, and executive presence
  • Business dining, formal receptions, and professional networking
  • Global leadership and cross-cultural communication
  • Korean and Korean–American business communication

Using Employer Education Benefits

Some employers offer education assistance or tuition reimbursement programs. Eligibility, approval, and reimbursement are determined by each employer's own policies and processes.

Employees should confirm with their employer before enrolling. BPCTI does not represent any course as pre-approved by a particular employer, and education benefits or reimbursement are not guaranteed.
