Expanding Access to Professional Education

BPCTI believes that access to professional education can create opportunities for personal growth, professional development, and stronger communities.

As part of the educational mission of Abigail Ruth Sung Foundation, BPCTI is committed to helping expand access to professional communication training for individuals from diverse backgrounds.

Scholarships & Tuition Assistance

Limited need-based scholarships and institutional tuition assistance may be available to eligible students who demonstrate financial need.

Scholarship and tuition assistance availability is not guaranteed and may vary by program and available funding.

Request Scholarship Information

Awards are subject to:

  • Eligibility requirements
  • Demonstrated financial need
  • Available scholarship funding
  • Program availability
  • BPCTI scholarship policies

Help Open Doors Through Education

Your support can help expand access to professional communication education and create opportunities for learners who may otherwise face financial barriers to professional training.

BP Communication Training Institute (BPCTI) is a DBA of Abigail Ruth Sung Foundation, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization.

Support Our Scholarship Fund

Contributions may help support:

  • Need-based scholarships
  • Tuition assistance opportunities
