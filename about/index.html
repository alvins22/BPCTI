About BPCTI

Professional Communication. Greater Opportunity. Stronger Connections.

BP Communication Training Institute is a professional communication and career development training institution offering specialized instruction in English and Korean business communication.

Who We Are

Communication That Advances Careers

BPCTI's distinctive educational value is the integration of professional language, career communication, executive presence, workplace etiquette, business dining, formal networking, and cross-cultural leadership.

We believe effective communication can strengthen professional confidence, improve workplace collaboration, bridge cultural differences, and create new opportunities for personal and professional growth.

Our Korean program is an authentic professional development option for Korean American professionals and heritage Korean speakers, built around formal business communication rather than introductory language study.

Our Mission

Empowering People Through Communication

Our mission is to expand access to high-quality professional communication education and empower individuals with the practical skills, confidence, and cultural understanding needed to communicate effectively in today's diverse and global workforce.

Our Educational Philosophy

Effective Communication Is Not Developed Through Theory Alone

At BPCTI, students learn through instruction, practice, interaction, feedback, and assessment.

Our training emphasizes practical application through professional simulations, so learners develop communication skills they can use in professional and real-world environments.

Our Nonprofit Foundation

Education with Purpose

BP Communication Training Institute (BPCTI) is a DBA of Abigail Ruth Sung Foundation, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization.

Abigail Ruth Sung Foundation was established on February 2, 2022, to provide educational and community-based programs that expand learning opportunities and serve the community.

BPCTI extends this educational mission through structured professional communication and career development training for adult learners and working professionals.
