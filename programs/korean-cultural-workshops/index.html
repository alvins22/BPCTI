Optional Cultural Workshops

Korean Cultural Experience Workshops

Experience Korean Culture. Discover Traditions. Connect Through Shared Experiences.

Alongside its professional communication programs, BPCTI offers optional workshops that invite participants to experience Korean culture firsthand through food, traditional attire, celebration, and performance.

Request Workshop Information
Workshop Categories

Four Ways to Experience Korean Culture

Workshop dates, formats, and availability are announced as workshops are scheduled. Contact BPCTI to ask about upcoming workshops or group sessions.

01

Korean Culinary Culture & Dining Experience

Explore Korean food traditions through guided cooking activities, dining etiquette, and cultural conversation.

02

Hanbok & Korean Formal Event Etiquette

Discover traditional Korean attire, formal greetings, cultural celebrations, and appropriate conduct at special events.

03

Hanbok Styling, Hair & Makeup Workshop

Learn traditional-inspired styling techniques, accessories, hair preparation, and makeup appropriate for formal Korean cultural occasions.

04

K-Pop Performance & Social Confidence Workshop

Learn selected K-Pop choreography, develop stage confidence, and prepare a group performance for a cultural or social event.

Optional and separate. Cultural workshops are optional enrichment experiences. They are separate from BPCTI's eight professional communication specializations and are not part of any specialization's curriculum or Certificate of Completion requirements.
