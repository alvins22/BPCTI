Korean Business Dining, Formal Receptions & Professional Networking

Build Meaningful Business Relationships Through Cultural Fluency.

This specialization prepares participants to communicate effectively during Korean business luncheons, client dinners, formal banquets, evening receptions, corporate celebrations, and professional networking events.

Course Overview

About This Specialization

It focuses on the relationship between business communication, hospitality, social etiquette, professional hierarchy, and culturally appropriate interaction.

The course is especially relevant to Korean American professionals who may be comfortable with Korean food and family customs but want greater confidence in formal corporate dining and business social situations.

This is one of four independent specializations in the Korean Global Business Communication program. You may enroll in it without completing any other specialization.

Who Should Enroll?

  • Korean American business professionals
  • Entrepreneurs engaging with Korean companies
  • Professionals hosting Korean clients or executives
  • Heritage Korean speakers seeking formal business etiquette training
  • Individuals attending Korean corporate dinners and receptions
Learning Outcomes

What You Will Be Able to Do

Upon successful completion, participants should be able to:

  • Navigate Korean professional introductions
  • Respond appropriately to dining invitations
  • Meet seating and hosting expectations
  • Sustain business conversation during meals
  • Interact confidently at formal receptions
  • Conduct professional follow-up
Course Format & Instructional Approach

How This Course Works

Instruction combines guided teaching with professional simulations, role-play, coaching, applied assignments, and performance assessments. The same overall instructional duration does not mean that every student receives identical content or that every specialization is taught at a single language level.

Practical Training Experiences

Applied Training Highlights

Every week pairs a learning focus with practical training, so participants rehearse the real professional situations this specialization prepares them for.

  • Korean business dining simulations
  • Formal introductions
  • Banquet and reception etiquette
  • Executive hosting
  • Korean-language networking
Please note: Korean business practices vary by organization, generation, industry, and individual preference. Instruction emphasizes situational judgment rather than rigid cultural stereotypes. Participation in alcohol-related customs is never required.
Placement & Proficiency Guidance

Choose Your Specialization. Find Your Appropriate Training Level.

BPCTI recognizes that professional communication needs differ according to language proficiency, work experience, and career objectives.

Applicants select the specialization that best matches their professional goals. A placement and training-needs assessment helps BPCTI recommend an appropriate instructional level or class.

Depending on the specialization, assessment may consider:

  • Current communication proficiency
  • Formal versus conversational language skills
  • Professional and leadership experience
  • Relevant workplace communication needs
  • Career development objectives
  • Readiness for applied professional training
Selecting a specialization does not require completing another specialization. Placement recommendations and any applicable proficiency prerequisites are communicated before enrollment.
Heritage Korean speakers and fluent Korean American professionals are not automatically placed in beginner instruction. Their formal professional Korean and relevant business communication competencies are assessed directly.
Assessment & Certificate

Demonstrate Your Professional Communication Skills

Assessment may include participation in applied exercises, structured communication assignments, instructor feedback, and a final performance-based assessment aligned with the specialization's learning outcomes.

Students who fulfill the published course completion requirements receive a BPCTI Certificate of Completion identifying the specialization completed.

BPCTI Certificates of Completion document completion of BPCTI training. They are not academic degrees, state-issued professional licenses, industry certifications, or guarantees of employment. Attendance, assessment, and certificate eligibility requirements are provided in the applicable BPCTI catalog.
