Programs

Professional Communication Training for Career Advancement and Global Business

Build the communication competencies that support professional success—from high-stakes interviews and executive meetings to business dining, formal receptions, and cross-cultural leadership.

BP Communication Training Institute offers two distinctive programs and eight career-focused specializations. Choose the professional skills you want to develop, then receive placement guidance based on your communication proficiency, experience, and goals.

Two Distinctive Programs

Professional English Business Communication

Communicate Your Value. Strengthen Your Professional Presence.

Strengthen the skills needed to communicate effectively in American and international professional environments. Develop confidence and precision in interviews, meetings, presentations, professional social settings, and leadership interactions.

View English Specializations

Korean Global Business Communication

Bridging Cultures. Elevating Professional Communication.

Advance your effectiveness in Korean and Korean-American professional environments. Develop business communication, cultural fluency, executive etiquette, and relationship-building skills appropriate to your experience and language proficiency.

View Korean Specializations
Eight Specializations

Find Your Professional Specialization

Each program offers four independent specializations. Students select the specialization relevant to their professional objectives; a placement and training-needs assessment helps identify an appropriate instructional group or proficiency level.

Why BPCTI?

What Makes BPCTI Different?

Career-Relevant Communication

Training is organized around professional situations and workplace performance rather than language drills alone.

Experiential Professional Training

Participants practice realistic interviews, virtual meetings, professional introductions, formal dining conversations, and cross-cultural business scenarios.

In Development

Flexible Learning for Working Professionals

BPCTI is developing flexible learning options designed to accommodate rotating shifts, overtime schedules, and the professional responsibilities of working adults.

Learn about the proposed learning model
  • Morning and afternoon class options
  • Week-by-week recorded lesson access
  • Online learning materials and assignments
  • Make-up learning opportunities for missed sessions
  • Instructor feedback and performance-based assessments
  • Individual student progress tracking

These options are currently in development and are not yet available. Class times, formats, and availability will be announced once scheduling, technology, instructional policies, and applicable approvals are finalized.
